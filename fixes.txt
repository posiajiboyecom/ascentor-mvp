Confirmation dialogs

New dialog: confirmAction() in components/Modal.tsx works anywhere. It reads the wording to pick the title and button label, and shows a red button for destructive actions. It focuses Cancel first, closes on Escape, and returns focus afterwards.
Converted: all 27 browser confirm() popups now use it.

Sage

Orb: a floating gold orb in the member app.
Greeting: once per login session it plays a soft chime, shows a typing indicator, and types a personalised greeting by time of day. It has a "Talk to Sage" button.
Controls: the greeting can be muted (remembered), and it respects reduced-motion. It's hidden on /coach.
Browser sound rules: browsers block audio until the user has interacted with the page. If the chime is blocked, it plays on your first click or keypress within 10 seconds instead. So the sound may not play the instant the page loads after a fresh login.
Sign-out: signing out resets it, so the next login is greeted again.
Things to know
Sage is not an actual assistant panel. It's a greeting and a shortcut into the existing coach page. If you want in-place chat from the orb, that's a separate feature.
Admin sign-out: the admin dashboard has the same black sidebar and confirmation. Sage is member-only, so admins won't see it.
Old legacy pages are covered too: the older app/nav/* pages lost their dark toggle and now confirm on sign-out. I don't know if they're still in use.
Marketing pages were not restyled. They had no theme toggle, and any dark sections on them are part of their own design.
Docs: README, wiki and CHANGELOG are updated for all of the above.
